About This Farm

My name is Emmanuel Ssekandi and I have been farming in Kayunga District since 2008, when I inherited 12 acres from my late father. What started as a small mixed plot has grown into a dedicated fruit farm — our specialty is Kayunga pineapples, which are known across Kampala for their consistent sweetness and size.

We grow without chemical pesticides, relying instead on compost and crop rotation. Every fruit that leaves our farm has been hand-inspected. We deliver directly to restaurants in Kampala twice a week — Tuesdays and Fridays — so your kitchen receives produce that was harvested within 48 hours.

We have supplied Kati Kati, Serena Hotel, and Café Javas consistently for over a year. I personally ensure quality on every delivery.
