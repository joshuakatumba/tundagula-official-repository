How AgriConnect works

Four steps from farm gate to restaurant kitchen

01
👨‍🌾
Farmer registers & lists produce
Farmers create verified profiles, list their produce with real-time pricing and stock, and publish their harvest calendar so restaurants can plan ahead.
02
🔍
Restaurant browses & orders
Restaurants search verified farmers by produce, location, and rating. They place direct purchase orders with delivery date and quantity — no phone tag, no brokers.
03
🔒
Escrow payment via MoMo
Restaurants pay via MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. Funds are held in escrow by AgriConnect and released to the farmer only after delivery is confirmed — protecting both parties.
04
🚚
Deliver, confirm, repeat
Farmer delivers directly to the restaurant. The restaurant confirms receipt, triggers payment release, and both sides rate each other — building a trust record over time.
